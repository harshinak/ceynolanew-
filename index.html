Tree to Tin

Six steps, all done by hand

Every quill in your tin passed through the same six stages our family has followed for generations.

01

Harvest

Bark is cut from two-year shoots at first light, when the sap runs easiest.

02

Peel

The outer skin is scraped away by hand, leaving the pale inner bark exposed.

03

Roll

While still soft, the bark curls into a quill; smaller pieces nest inside the larger ones.

04

Dry

Quills rest in the shade for days, tightening and darkening to their true colour.

05

Grade

Each quill, feathering and chip is sorted by hand for size and quality.

06

Pack

Sealed fresh into tins and pouches, ready to leave Sri Lanka.

Our Story

A family plot, not a factory

Ceynola Cinnamon started on a small plot of land where cinnamon has been peeled and rolled by hand for generations. We kept it that way on purpose — no cassia mixed in to stretch the yield, no machine-shaved bark. Just true Cinnamomum verum, harvested and rolled the slow way, then packed and sent out ourselves.
