Full Transparency

From our family plot to your package.

Traceability isn't a buzzword for us — it's our family legacy. We believe you should know exactly where your cinnamon comes from, and whose hands crafted it.

Rooted in Sri Lankan Soil

One plot, watched over tree by tree

Unlike industrial cinnamon, Ceynola is grown on a small-scale family plot. This lets us monitor every single tree, keeping the soil healthy and harvesting bark only when it reaches its peak potency.

The Journey of a Quill

Tracked from harvest to your door

Every batch is tracked from the moment of harvest to the moment it leaves our door.

01

The Harvest

We identify the most mature stems, ensuring the inner bark is supple and rich in essential oils.

02

The Peeling

Using traditional tools, our artisans peel the outer bark with precision to avoid damaging the quill.

03

The Rolling

The cinnamon is hand-rolled into tight quills — this is where the Ceynola signature quality forms.

04

Air-Drying

We air-dry our cinnamon in controlled environments to lock in the volatile oils and aroma.

05

Grading

Every batch is sorted by hand into quills, featherings or chips, based on thickness and colour.
